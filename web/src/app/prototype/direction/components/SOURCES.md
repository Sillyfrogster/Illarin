# Component sources

Button and Popover are adapted from shadcn/ui's New York registry, retrieved
September 8, 2026. The scoped Tailwind prefix, colours, 44px targets and portal
container keep the prototype independent of production styles.

- https://ui.shadcn.com/r/styles/new-york/button.json
- https://ui.shadcn.com/r/styles/new-york/popover.json
- https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/LICENSE.md

NotchNav adapts Vengeance UI's three-slice notch composition and mobile reveal.
It removes the source's doubled hairline outlines and connects the navigation
to Illarin's routes. PerspectiveCarousel adapts its perspective slide track,
bounded selection and spring transitions, adding touch dragging, original-aspect
media, accessible controls and a reduced-motion alternative.

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ashutoshx7/VengeanceUI/main/public/r/notch-navbar.json
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ashutoshx7/VengeanceUI/main/public/r/perspective-carousel.json
- https://github.com/Ashutoshx7/VengeanceUI/blob/main/LICENSE
